“Good day. I will demonstrate the RHU administrator’s workflow, following the order of the modules in the side menu.”

**1. Overview**

[Click Overview.]

“The administrator starts by reviewing summary figures, consultation trends, and recent activity.

On this page, the administrator also processes certificate requests by reviewing the request, assigning the responsible staff, and requesting e-signature approval. After the required approval, the administrator releases the certificate using Approve Final & Send.

The administrator can also open resident messages, review their concerns, and send replies.”

**2. Landing Page Content**

[Click Landing Page Content.]

“Next, the administrator manages public information by publishing announcements and health events. This section also maintains municipal officials’ information and event gallery photos. Existing entries can be reviewed or removed.”

**3. User Management**

[Click User Management.]

“The administrator registers healthcare staff accounts and assigns their positions. In the account list, the administrator can update roles, activate or deactivate accounts, and remove accounts when necessary.”

**4. Staff Accounts**

[Click Staff Accounts.]

“The administrator reviews staff folders and assigned resident records. Through Staff Account Tools, the administrator updates personnel details. Password changes require a verification code sent to the staff member’s registered email.”

**5. Resident Registry**

[Click Resident Registry.]

“The administrator searches for residents and reviews their registered information. Resident Registry Tools allows the administrator to create or update a resident record. The administrator can also activate or deactivate a resident’s status.”

**6. Emergency Referrals**

[Click Emergency Referrals.]

“The administrator reviews incoming emergency requests, including the resident’s contact information, reported emergency, and pickup location. These details support coordination with the appropriate personnel.”


“**7. OPD Consultations**

[Click OPD Consultations, then View records.]

“The administrator uses this section to review patient appointments and consultation history, including schedules, assigned healthcare providers, and clinical information.
The administrator’s role here is to monitor and review records, not to edit them. Updates to the diagnosis, treatment details, and consultation status are handled by the assigned healthcare provider.”

**8. Maternal Health**

[Click Maternal Health.]

“The administrator reviews pregnancy cases, risk information, family planning records, and maternal referrals. A new pregnancy case can be created through the tools form, which calculates the expected delivery date from the last menstrual period. Existing case and referral statuses can also be updated.”

**9. Immunization**

[Click Immunization.]

“The administrator reviews vaccination history and follow-up dates. Through Immunization Tools, the administrator selects the resident and provider, enters vaccination details, and saves a new or updated record.”

**10. Disease Surveillance**

[Click Disease Surveillance.]

“The administrator monitors recorded disease cases. Through the tools form, the administrator selects a resident and disease, records symptoms and the case date, and classifies the case as suspected, probable, or confirmed.”

**11. Medicine Inventory**

[Click Medicine Inventory.]

“The administrator checks medicine quantities, reorder levels, and expiry dates. New supplies are recorded using Save Item, while stock adjustments are submitted through the available controls and Apply button.”

**12. Vital Statistics**

[Click Vital Statistics.]

“The administrator reviews birth and death records. Through Vital Record Tools, the administrator selects the record type, enters the required information, and clicks Create Vital Record.”

**13. Reports & Analytics**

[Click Reports & Analytics.]

“The administrator applies filters and reviews service trends, barangay information, resident distribution, and staff summaries. The Report Generator prepares an FHSIS report for the selected month and year.”

**14. Audit Logs**

[Click Audit Logs.]

“The administrator reviews recorded actions to trace system activity. Search and paging help locate a particular action, account, or record.”

**15. System Settings**

[Click System Settings.]

“The administrator updates facility information and email settings. Send Test Email checks email delivery. System Tools provides table maintenance and a downloadable SQL database backup.”

**16. Security**

[Click Security.]

“Finally, the administrator can change their password by entering the current password, new password, and confirmation. This section also displays two-factor authentication information and the role-based permissions overview.”

“That completes the administrator’s workflow, following the order of the side menu.”
