"Good day. I will demonstrate the RHU nurse's workflow, following the order of the modules in the side menu."

**1. Overview**

[Click Overview.]

"The nurse starts by reviewing the summary figures for received OPD consultations, diagnosed patients, child immunizations, and active TB cases.

The disease surveillance alert provides quick access to the surveillance page through View Surveillance.

In the Received Resident Consultation & Triage Queue, the nurse searches for a resident and selects a consultation. The details panel shows the resident's information, chief complaint, consultation date, clinical notes, and current status. Expanding Consultation Response displays the recorded assessment and response details."

**2. OPD Triage**

[Click OPD Triage. Select a resident in the nursing triage form.]

"Next, the nurse records the resident's chief complaint and vital signs, including the available blood pressure, temperature, and other measurement fields. Clicking Save Nursing Triage Vitals saves the nursing triage record.

The nurse also reviews consultations in the Triage & Nursing Assessment Log. After selecting a record, the nurse can enter the clinical assessment, update the available consultation details and status, and click Save Changes. Saved updates are reflected in the corresponding resident and administrator records."

**3. Patient Records**

[Click Patient Records. Open a resident's record or the medical record form.]

"The nurse reviews resident medical profiles, including blood type, allergies, medical history, and recorded measurements.

Through the medical record form, the nurse selects a resident and updates the available profile and health information. Some fields are locked according to the record's existing information. After completing the editable fields, the nurse clicks Save to store the changes."

**4. Immunization**

[Click Immunization, then Record Vaccine.]

"The nurse reviews the immunization monitoring summary, which shows administered records, doses due this month, and overdue vaccines.

To record a vaccination, the nurse selects the resident and enters the required vaccination details, including the vaccine, age group, batch, date given, and next dose date when applicable. Clicking Save Record stores the vaccination entry.

The list displays the resident's vaccination information and next visit date to support follow-up monitoring."

**5. Nutrition (OPT+)**

[Click Nutrition (OPT+).]

"The nurse reviews the Operation Timbang Plus nutrition records. Each entry shows the resident's information, barangay, height, weight, calculated BMI, and the status displayed by the system.

For entries with a status other than Normal, the Alert Resident button sends a notification asking the resident to contact the Rural Health Unit for a nutrition assessment."

**6. TB-DOTS**

[Click TB-DOTS.]

"The nurse reviews recorded tuberculosis cases in the TB-DOTS monitoring section. Each entry displays the resident's information, record source, registration reference, classification, treatment status, barangay, and record date.

These details help the nurse review the recorded cases and coordinate follow-up with the appropriate healthcare personnel."

**7. Disease Surveillance**

[Click Disease Surveillance.]

"Finally, the nurse reviews disease surveillance summaries. Each disease entry shows the recorded case count, confirmed and suspected or probable cases, latest case date, affected barangays, and recorded statuses.

This section helps the nurse monitor the disease information recorded in the system and identify cases that require coordination and follow-up."

"That completes the nurse's workflow, following the order of the side menu."
